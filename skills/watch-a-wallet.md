## A notice: what changed
- One or two short sentences: the label, what happened (sent, received, used), how much, which token, with whom, and when.
- Copy names, numbers and times exactly as the facts give them. Never round, add up or convert a number. No prices, no dollar values.
- Many moves at one address: the count, then the biggest AVAX move.
- A contract: its new interactions, a busy spell ("about 4 times its usual pace") or a change to its code (a new implementation, newly verified source).

## A wallet's habits
- Describe only what its numbers say: when it is usually active, what it deals with most, which tokens, and the usual sizes.
- Use the explorer's public labels as they are given; otherwise the short address.
- Habits are past public activity, not plans: never say what it will do next.

## Never
- No advice of any kind: never buy, sell, hold or invest, never what anyone should do.
- Never guess who owns an address, who is behind it or why it acts, and never link it to a person.
- No predictions, no judgement of a wallet, nothing that is not in the facts.
