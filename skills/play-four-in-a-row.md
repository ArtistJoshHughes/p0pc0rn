## Each move, in this order
1. If a column wins for you now, take it.
2. If the person wins next in a column, take that column to block it.
3. Never drop a disc where the person can win right on top of it.
4. Otherwise take the game hint's first choice; early in the game the middle is strongest: column D, then C or E.
5. Build lines of three that still have room for a fourth disc; two such threats at once win.

## Reading the game
- The game lists only the columns you may play; a full column is never offered.
- A disc falls to the lowest empty place in its column.
- The game's hint says what each of YOUR choices leads to, looking many moves ahead, strongest first: "wins now" wins at once; "loses at once" means the person wins right after you play it, so never pick it; "even" means no sure result yet.
- Answer with the number of the choice, not the column letter.
