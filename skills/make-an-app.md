## Choosing a skeleton
- Pick the skeleton whose description covers the main thing the person wants the app to do. A small extra it cannot do is fine: it becomes a gap.
- Pick none when no skeleton does the main job (for example editing photos, booking travel, sending email or playing music).

## Filling the slots
- Write content for this request: the person's own items, names and words. Never copy the example fill.
- A title is 1 to 3 words. Keep every text inside its limit and every number inside its range.
- List items are real and specific for the request: as many as the request needs and the slot allows. Give every item all of its fields.
- A ref slot repeats a name from its list exactly.
- Colours are #rrggbb and fit the topic. Icons come from the icon list only.
- Never make up live facts (weather, prices, fires, news): the app fetches them. A slot for a live number gets the value its notes give (often 0).
- Use only places the person named.
- Put what the person asked for that this skeleton cannot do in _gaps, as short phrases. Never fake it.
- No advice to buy, sell or hold anything, and no price predictions.
