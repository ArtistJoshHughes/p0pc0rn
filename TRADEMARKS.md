# p0pc0rn name and icon policy

> For the repository, not part of the app's UI. Revised October 14, 2026. Not legal advice.

## The mark

- **p0pc0rn™** and the popcorn icon are used as an unregistered trademark by Joshua Hughes. The icon covers 🍿 and the
  app icon files `icon-180.png`, `icon-512.png` and `icon-1024.png`.
- There is no USPTO application or registration. The mark is written with ™, not ®, because it is not registered.
- The p0pc0rn Non-Commercial License (`LICENSE.md`) sets the rules for the code. This file sets the rules for the name
  and the icon.

## Forks and modified versions get their own name

This is the usual rule for open projects. It means a changed version is never mistaken for the real p0pc0rn.

- **Pick your own name and icon.** A modified version you share must not be called "p0pc0rn", must not use the popcorn
  icon, and must not use a name or icon confusingly similar to them (`LICENSE.md` section 3.3).
- **Credit the original.** Keep the line "Based on p0pc0rn by Joshua Hughes", the copyright notice, `LICENSE.md` and
  `NOTICE`. Show the credit, with a link to the official site, in your version's own screens and at the top of its
  README, for as long as copyright lasts; this also covers any part, port or translation (`LICENSE.md` section 3.6). You
  may say truthfully that your version is "based on p0pc0rn".
- **Say it's modified.** Make clear it is not the Official Distribution.
- **Contributions** accepted into the Official Distribution are part of p0pc0rn and keep the name.

## The safeguards stay in every build

Every build, official or modified, keeps all of these, working and unchanged in substance (`LICENSE.md` section 3.4):

1. **The lesson:** the copyright, trademark and likeness lesson, shown before the first render and again whenever the
   terms version changes.
2. **The agreement gate:** the "I agree" card, and the engine check that refuses every picture and video job without
   the agreement for the current terms version (engine `TERMS_VERSION`, error `NO_CONSENT`).
3. **The upload checkbox:** the attestation box on every image or video input. It starts unchecked on every new upload
   and blocks Make until it is ticked.

Translations and restyling are fine as long as the meaning stays the same.

## Serving

Only Joshua Hughes serves p0pc0rn (`LICENSE.md` section 3.2). Everyone else may run their own copy only for themselves.
No one else may use the name or the icon on a hosted copy, a store listing, a service or merchandise.
