1. **Infringe copyright.** Do not reproduce, adapt, distribute, display or perform someone else's work without the owner's permission or a valid defense such as fair use (17 U.S.C. §§ 106, 107). Fair use is a defense argued after the fact, not a permission.
2. **Infringe trademarks.** Do not make output that uses other people's logos, brands or characters used as brands in ways that break U.S. trademark law. Do not reproduce watermarks or logos of others in outputs.
3. **Use someone's likeness without consent.** Do not use the face, body or voice of an identifiable person without their consent (unless it is you). Several states protect likeness and voice (e.g., Tennessee's ELVIS Act, California's digital-replica statutes).
4. **Make deepfakes, voice clones or lip-syncs of a person without their consent.** Do not make a real person appear to say or do something, clone their voice, or lip-sync their face, unless that person has consented (or it is you). This is also LTX-2 Attachment A item 7.
5. **Make non-consensual intimate imagery.** Do not create or share non-consensual intimate images of anyone, including AI deepfakes. Publishing them is a federal crime under the TAKE IT DOWN Act.
6. **Strip attribution.** Do not remove watermarks, signatures, credits or other copyright management information to hide infringement (17 U.S.C. § 1202).
7. **Upload media you have no rights to.** Use only inputs you own, or that you have written permission to use for AI-generated derivative works, as the in-app upload attestation states.

The assistant's lookups (Wikipedia and Fandom) may inform its answers and lyrics, but any picture or video it plans from them is an original take inspired by the subject: it never recreates a real person, a character, a creature, a brand, a logo, an artwork or a product, and it never fetches images.

## Say that it is AI-generated

When you share or publish anything you made with p0pc0rn, disclose that it is AI-generated (for example "Made with AI using p0pc0rn").
This is a condition of the LTX-2 licence (Attachment A item 5: content must not be placed anywhere "without expressly and intelligibly
disclaiming that the information and/or content is machine generated"), and ACE-Step asks the same for songs. Every file p0pc0rn saves
or shares carries the label "AI-generated with p0pc0rn" in its standard metadata and nothing else; do not remove it to pass the file off
as human-made.
